# Hydra Project Wiki

This is the human-facing route into Hydra. The root [README](/README.md)
explains why Hydra exists and what is implemented; this wiki helps evaluators,
contributors, daily users, and maintainers reach the right depth without
starting in framework internals.

Maintainers can trace durable claims through the [Source Map](/project-wiki/hydra-framework/reference/source-map.md#maintainer-evidence).

## Source Of Truth

`.hydra-framework/` owns canonical framework rules, task state, knowledge
packages, capabilities, adapters, scripts, and validation evidence.
`project-wiki/` explains and routes that material for humans. Durable claims
must link to verified code, project docs, task records, or other source
material.
