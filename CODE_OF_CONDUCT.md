# Code of Conduct

Hydra is a collaborative project for people and AI agents. We want discussion
and contributions to be constructive, respectful, and focused on improving the
project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate in issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.
- Discuss ideas and work, not personal characteristics.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and the publication of private
information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe a violation, contact the repository owner through
the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive
personal details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as privately and
fairly as practical.

## Maintenance

This is a deliberately small starter policy. Maintainers may expand it with a
dedicated contact, enforcement process, and adoption date as the community
grows.
